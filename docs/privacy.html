Privacy Policy

QuickBall
Developer: Chayan Mistry
Effective date: 14 October 2026

This privacy policy explains how the QuickBall Android app ("QuickBall", "the app") handles your information. QuickBall is a floating assistive button that gives you quick access to navigation actions, system controls and app shortcuts.

  • QuickBall does not collect, store on a server, sell or share your personal information.
  • The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting.
  • The app does not request internet access. Your settings stay on your device.
  • Optional tips in the Google Play version are processed by Google Play. The developer never sees your payment details.

1. Information stored on your device

QuickBall saves the following on your device only, so the app works the way you set it up:

  • Appearance and behavior settings, such as ball size, colors, menu layout and gestures
  • The shortcuts you choose for the menu, including the names of apps you add as shortcuts
  • The list of apps where you choose to hide the ball

This information is not sent anywhere and is not included in Android cloud backups. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

2. Accessibility Service

QuickBall uses the Android Accessibility Service API. This is required for the app's core features and is turned on only when you enable it in your device's Accessibility settings.

QuickBall uses it to:

  • Show the floating ball and its menu on top of other apps
  • Perform actions you trigger from the menu or with gestures: Back, Home, Recent apps, open notifications, open quick settings, lock the screen, take a screenshot and open the power menu
  • Detect which app is currently open (by its package name), so the ball can be hidden automatically in the apps you select

QuickBall does not use the Accessibility Service to read the content of your screen, the text you type, your messages, passwords or any other personal data. The name of the app in the foreground is used only at that moment to decide whether to show or hide the ball. It is not recorded, stored or shared.

3. Permissions

QuickBall asks only for the permissions its features need:

Screenshots taken from the menu are created and saved by the Android system. QuickBall does not open, read or upload them.

4. Tips and payments

QuickBall is free. All features are available without paying. Supporting the developer is optional, and a tip does not unlock any feature.

Google Play version. You can leave a one-time tip through Google Play Billing. The payment is handled entirely by Google. QuickBall and the developer never receive your card number, bank details or other payment information. Google provides the developer with limited transaction information, such as an order number, the item purchased, the price, the date and your country, which is used only for accounting and refunds. Google's handling of your payment is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

Versions distributed outside Google Play (for example on GitHub or F-Droid). Support links open external websites such as Gumroad and Ko-fi in your browser. Any payment you make there is governed by that website's own privacy policy. If you send a tip by bKash, the transfer happens in the bKash app, and the developer receives the information bKash shows to any recipient, such as your bKash number and the amount.

5. Data sharing

QuickBall does not share any information with third parties, because it does not collect any. The developer does not sell, rent or trade user data.

6. Children's privacy

QuickBall is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

7. Security

Because QuickBall keeps your settings on your device and does not transmit them, your information is protected by your device's own security, such as your screen lock and Android's app sandbox.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app changes. The effective date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
